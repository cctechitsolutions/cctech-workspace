Privacy Policy

CCTech Business Workspace and connected messaging channels

Effective: 16 November 2026

CCTech IT Solutions, operated by Chandrashekar, uses the CCTech Business Workspace to manage sales enquiries, technical support, vendor sourcing, quotations and orders. This policy describes information handled through that workspace and our connected Facebook Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp channels when those connections are active.

Information we receive

  • Your messages, enquiry details and attachments you choose to send, including device models, specifications, photos and descriptions of faults.
  • Identifiers supplied by the messaging platform, such as your channel user ID or WhatsApp number, and message IDs, timestamps and delivery information.
  • Your name, optional contact details, budget, delivery or service location, and other information you provide for your request.
  • Business records associated with your enquiry, such as supplier offers, approved quotations, order choices, payment-verification notes, fulfilment updates and review requests.
  • Staff account information and technical records needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the workspace.

How we use information

We use this information to understand requirements, respond to questions, arrange technical support, check compatible products and supplier availability, prepare quotations, manage orders and payments, coordinate fulfilment and handle follow-up requests. Rule-based automated replies can collect details and route messages to the team. Staff review uncertain requirements and approve customer quotations; the owner verifies reported payments.

Attachments may require human review. Automated messages do not establish a diagnosis, guarantee availability, approve a quotation or verify that funds have arrived.

Who may receive information

  • Authorised CCTech owner and staff who need it to handle your request.
  • Relevant suppliers or service partners who receive the specifications needed to assess availability, compatibility or work. Vendor price enquiries are intended to exclude your identity, contact details, delivery address and budget. Please avoid including unrelated private information in device descriptions or photos.
  • Delivery or service providers, where details are needed to arrange a fulfilment option you request.
  • Service providers used to operate the workspace, including Supabase for authentication, business records and server functions, GitHub Pages for hosting the application interface, and Meta for connected messaging services. Those providers may process technical information under their own policies.

We may also disclose relevant records where required by applicable law or to address fraud, security incidents or a dispute.

Storage and access

The application interface is publicly hosted. Business records are stored separately and access is restricted through workspace accounts and database permissions. Server credentials are kept separately from public application files. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Information may be processed in the countries where our service providers operate. The information you send through Meta platforms is also subject to those platforms' policies.

Retention

We retain information as needed to handle enquiries, provide services, support orders and warranties, maintain necessary business records and resolve disputes. Retention can vary by record type and applicable requirements. You can contact us to request deletion or information about the records associated with you. Records that must be retained for legal obligations, security or an unresolved dispute may be restricted or retained for those purposes.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may ask us to access, correct or delete information associated with you, or ask the team to stop automated follow-up. Email cctechitsolutions@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify that a request concerns your own records. See our data-deletion instructions.

Deleting CCTech's workspace records does not itself delete messages from your phone, Meta account, a recipient's device or another provider's systems. Those platforms offer their own controls.

Information you should not send

Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, complete payment-card details or unrelated personal documents in a chat. If service work requires access to a device or account, arrange the appropriate process directly with the team.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our handling of information changes.
