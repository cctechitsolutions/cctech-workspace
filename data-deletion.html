CCTECH IT SOLUTIONS

Data-deletion instructions

Effective: 16 November 2026

You can request deletion of information associated with your enquiries, messaging conversations or orders in the CCTech Business Workspace.

How to request deletion

  1. Email cctechitsolutions@gmail.com with the subject CCTech data deletion request.
  2. Identify the channel you used: WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger or Instagram. Include the phone number or account identifier used to contact CCTech, and an enquiry/order reference if available. State whether you want all eligible records removed or only specific information.
  3. The CCTech team will check that the request relates to your own information. Where needed, we may ask you to confirm through the original contact channel. Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or payment-card details.
  4. After checking the request, the team will explain the action taken and any records that need to be retained for legal obligations, security or an unresolved dispute. A deletion request does not automatically cancel an order or settle a payment dispute.

What this covers

This process covers records controlled by CCTech in its business workspace. It does not remove messages or records held independently on your device, by Meta, by a supplier or by another service provider. Use those providers' own privacy and deletion controls for their records.
